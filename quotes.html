Payment Policy

A minimum of 75% of the total cost must be paid before production begins.

For Pickup/Collection, the remaining balance must be paid before or on pickup/collection.

For Delivery, the remaining balance must be paid before product dispatch/delivery.

Turnaround Time

Turnaround time varies depending on the design, order details, and workload. An estimated completion date will be communicated after your order has been confirmed.
